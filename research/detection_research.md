## Scam taxonomy (draft from PRODUCT_SPEC / RISK_RULES)
- OTP / credential requests
- Upfront "registration", "verification" or "training" fees
- Bank / UPI PIN requests ("to receive stipend")
- Unknown app / APK installs
- Urgency and fake deadlines
- Personal email or unknown number posing as HR
- Shortened / lookalike links
- Hinglish variants of all of the above

## Rules for this folder
- Fictional messages only — invented names, companies and `.test` / `.example` domains.
- Never add real student messages, real phone numbers or working links.
- Store cases in `sample_messages/` with an expected risk band and evidence phrase.
